# RapVideo AI: AI Rap Video Generator

**[RapVideo AI](https://rapvideo-ai.io)** turns any photo into a short, lip-synced rap video. There's nothing to film and no rapping skills needed.

👉 Try it: **https://rapvideo-ai.io**

## How it works

1. Upload a clear photo (or two for a duo).
2. Pick a scene and a beat.
3. Add a topic like "my golden retriever who steals socks", or paste your own bars.

The AI writes original lyrics, generates a new beat and vocals, and animates the face so it raps in sync. Most videos are ready in 2 to 4 minutes and download as an MP4.
